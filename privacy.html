Family Calendar — Privacy

Family Calendar is a private calendar for the Ahlersmeyer family and the relatives they invite. It is not a commercial service.

What it stores

Events (titles, dates, times, places, notes and attached schedules) and the email addresses of the family members who can edit the calendar. Relatives who add an event provide the name they type with it.

Google sign-in and Gmail

Editors sign in with Google so the calendar knows who they are. If an editor chooses to scan their email, the app reads their Gmail read-only, in their own browser, to find invitations and schedules, and shows what it found for them to approve. Email content is never stored or sent anywhere except the specific events the editor chooses to add.

Who can see it

Only the family editors and people they share the family link or code with. Private events are visible to editors only. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any other purpose.

Removing data

Editors can delete any event at any time. To remove your Google access, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions. Questions: contact Sam Ahlersmeyer.
